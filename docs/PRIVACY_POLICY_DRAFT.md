## What this app is

Kids Learning is an educational app for young children. It includes letters, numbers, animals, words, a keyboard explorer, music, quizzes, a daily challenge, achievements, and parent settings. The first release does not require an account.

## Information stored on the device

The app saves the following on the device where it is installed:

- which letters, numbers, animals, and words have been explored
- quiz counts, the current run of correct answers, and earned achievements
- the current day’s daily challenge progress
- parent settings, including sound, speech on or off, speech speed, volume, a selected device voice, theme, and reduced motion
- up to 8 photos that a parent chooses for My Photo Puzzle, with an optional name for each. A parent adds them through the device's own picture picker after a parent check. The app crops each photo to a square, makes it smaller, and saves it without its original metadata, such as location

On Android, this information is stored in the app’s private storage. The app does not send it to the developer, a server, an analytics service, or an advertising service.

Android backup for this app’s data is turned off. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes the saved information. Reset All Progress, after a parent answers a short question, deletes learning progress, the answer streak, achievements, the current daily challenge, and puzzle photos. A parent can also delete puzzle photos one at a time in the game. That reset does not change sound and speech settings.

## Information this app does not collect

This version does not collect or transmit:

- account details, contact details, or a child’s name
- precise or approximate location
- camera input or microphone recordings
- photos: puzzle photos chosen by a parent stay on the device and are never uploaded
- advertising identifiers
- analytics or crash reports to a remote service

Speech uses the device’s built-in speech synthesizer. Musical notes and game sounds are generated on the device. Lessons are packaged with the app and do not need a network connection. If a parent selects a device voice that the device itself says needs a network, that voice may not speak while offline. The recommended voice uses an on-device voice when one is available.

## Children

The app is intended for young children, with a parent settings area. The owner must say which age groups the store listing targets and must follow the privacy laws that apply to those children, including any parental-consent rules in the countries where the app is offered. This draft does not decide those age groups and does not claim that the app meets a particular law.

## Services this app does not include

This version has no ads, no in-app purchases, no social features, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDK. If a later version adds any of those, or any external link, this policy has to be updated first and a parent check must remain in front of leaving the app or making a purchase.
